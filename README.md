# HLBVN Smart Credit

**Business Challenge 2026 — AI in Finance and Banking Track**

## Project Overview

HLBVN Smart Credit is a functional prototype designed to demonstrate how financial signals, data-quality checks and policy-driven decision routing can support more efficient credit assessment for customers with limited traditional credit history.

The prototype focuses on a key lending challenge: turning fragmented financial information into a structured, explainable decision workflow while maintaining appropriate risk controls.

## Business Problem

Traditional credit assessment can be slow and costly, particularly when applicants have limited credit bureau history or their financial information requires additional verification.

The proposed approach aims to reduce unnecessary manual review while ensuring that uncertain or inconsistent cases can be escalated for human assessment.

## Proposed Solution

HLBVN Smart Credit combines three key capabilities:

- **Financial signal assessment:** evaluates the financial information available in a customer application.
- **Data-quality checks:** identifies inconsistent or potentially unreliable information.
- **Policy-driven decision routing:** directs cases towards approval, further review or an alternative outcome according to configured rules.

## How It Works

**1. Input**

The user submits a customer profile and the financial or transaction information required by the selected scenario.

**2. Process**

The prototype applies configured assessment rules and data-quality checks to the available information.

**3. Output**

The system displays a simulated credit outcome and, where applicable, review flags or the next recommended action.

## Responsible Lending

The proposed workflow emphasises data quality, appropriate customer consent, explainable decision rules and human review for cases requiring further assessment.

## Implementation Notes and Limitations

This submission is a demonstration prototype. Its current assessment logic is rule-based and uses configured demo scenarios. It is not a production banking system and does not establish a live connection to HLBVN systems, credit bureaus, e-wallets or payment providers.

Any performance improvements described in the project are proposed targets or planning estimates, not verified live operational results.
